3.1 Your encryption key and recovery PIN

Ulap encrypts your notes, your account and savings-goal names, and every amount and balance on your device before they reach our servers. We store only unreadable ciphertext for those fields and cannot read them — not even to help you with support. Dates and categories are not encrypted, because the app needs them to build your charts and summaries.

During setup you choose a recovery PIN, which protects a copy of your encryption key so you can restore your data on a new device. We never see or store your PIN.

If you forget your recovery PIN, we cannot recover your data for you — and no one else can. The only way forward is to permanently delete that data and start fresh. You are responsible for remembering your PIN and for keeping your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose. See Privacy Policy § 7 for how this works.
